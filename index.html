About Me

I am a molecular biologist and bioinformatician working at the intersection of microbial genomics, transcriptomics, metagenomics, microbial ecology, and plant–microbe interactions.

My research combines experimental molecular biology with computational approaches to understand microbial systems and biological interactions. I am also interested in developing reproducible and accessible bioinformatics tools for biological data analysis.

Research Interests

  • RNA-seq and transcriptomics
  • Metagenomics
  • Plant–microbe interactions
  • Microbial ecology
  • Bioinformatics software development

Software

BOTAS

Bacterial Operon and Transcriptome Analysis Suite

BOTAS is an open-source bioinformatics toolkit for bacterial RNA-seq analysis, including reference indexing, read alignment, gene quantification, operon inference, and analysis of circular bacterial genomes.
